Having discussed the basic theory of abstract operator systems, it is desirable for us to prove the following theorem. 

\begin{theorem}{The Choi-Effros Representation Theorem}{thm: the Choi Effros representation theorem}
Let $V \in \UniOpSys$. Then, there exists $H \in \Hilb$ and a concrete operator system $S \subseteq \Bs(H)$ such that $V$ and $S$ are isomorphic in $\UniOpSys$. 
\end{theorem}
